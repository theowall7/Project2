My project centers around the Appalachian barn quilt, which is a decorative panel or series of panels often found on barns and houses in Appalachian Virginia. I really wanted to use a modern technology like code to reflect on this centuries-old cultural tradition and bring it to life in a new way. It was really challenging to achieve randomness here, because while these patterns can seem random at first, they are actually incredibly mathematically precise, so my random aspects were limited in order to keep the spirit of the barn quilt.
